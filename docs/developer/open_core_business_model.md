# SPAGeo Open-Core Business Model

## Purpose

SPAGeo is developed using an open-core model with a freely available Community Edition and a proprietary Enterprise Edition.

The purpose of this model is to keep the core SPAGeo technology available to the open-source community while providing a sustainable path for commercial development, support, deployment, and enterprise-oriented capabilities.

## Community Edition

The intended license for the SPAGeo Community Edition is the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPLv3).

The Community Edition is intended to provide the open-source foundation of SPAGeo, including the core groundwater and aquifer engineering functionality that is designated for community use.

Use, modification, and redistribution of GPLv3-covered SPAGeo Community Edition code are governed by the terms of the GPLv3 license.

Users and organizations redistributing modified GPLv3-covered components must comply with the applicable GPLv3 requirements.

## Enterprise Edition

The SPAGeo Enterprise Edition is a proprietary commercial offering.

Enterprise-specific components, extensions, services, or capabilities may be distributed under separate commercial licensing terms and are not automatically covered by the GPLv3 license.

The Enterprise Edition may provide additional capabilities intended for organizations requiring commercial deployment, enterprise integration, professional support, or other capabilities designated as enterprise-only.

Commercial licensing terms, pricing, support arrangements, and the specific scope of enterprise-only functionality are defined separately from the GPLv3 Community Edition.

## Separation of Editions

The Community Edition and Enterprise Edition are maintained as distinct licensing offerings.

GPLv3-covered Community Edition code remains subject to the GPLv3 license.

Proprietary Enterprise Edition components are subject to their applicable commercial license terms.

The presence of an Enterprise Edition does not remove or restrict rights granted to users under the GPLv3 for code that is distributed as part of the Community Edition under that license.

## Third-Party Dependencies

SPAGeo may depend on third-party software, libraries, datasets, services, or APIs.

Each third-party dependency remains subject to its own applicable license, terms of use, attribution requirements, or service conditions.

The SPAGeo project does not grant rights to third-party components beyond the rights provided by their respective licenses or terms.

## Licensing Governance

The project maintainers determine which SPAGeo components are included in the GPLv3 Community Edition and which components are offered exclusively through the proprietary Enterprise Edition.

Any future change to the licensing status of a component should be documented explicitly and should preserve the applicable rights and obligations associated with previously released versions.

## Summary

| Edition | Licensing model | Intended role |
|---|---|---|
| Community Edition | GPLv3 | Open-source SPAGeo foundation |
| Enterprise Edition | Proprietary / commercial | Commercial and enterprise-specific capabilities |

This document describes the intended open-core business and licensing model. It does not replace the applicable software license, commercial agreement, or third-party terms governing a particular component or distribution.
