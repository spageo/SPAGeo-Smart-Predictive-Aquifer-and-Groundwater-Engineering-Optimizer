# SPAGeo Financial Projections

## Purpose

This document defines the five-year financial and growth projections supplied for the SPAGeo funding and investment strategy.

The figures in this document are planning projections. They are not historical financial results, audited statements, or independently verified forecasts. Actual results may differ based on product development, customer acquisition, pricing, market conditions, funding, operating costs, and execution.

## Projection Summary

| Year | Revenue | Users | Team Size |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | $100K | 200 | 5 |
| Year 2 | $500K | 800 | 10 |
| Year 3 | $2M | 2,500 | 20 |
| Year 4 | $5M | 6,000 | 35 |
| Year 5 | $10M | 15,000 | 50 |

## Revenue Projection

The supplied projection assumes revenue growth from **$100K in Year 1** to **$10M in Year 5**.

| Year | Projected Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | $100K |
| Year 2 | $500K |
| Year 3 | $2M |
| Year 4 | $5M |
| Year 5 | $10M |

These figures represent management planning assumptions and should be refined as pricing, customer conversion, enterprise contracts, recurring revenue, and other revenue drivers become better understood.

| Year | Projected Users |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | 200 |

| Year 4 | 6,000 |

User counts should be defined consistently when this projection is used externally, including whether the metric represents registered users, active users, paying users, organizations, or another customer metric.

## Team Growth Projection

The supplied projection assumes team expansion from **5 people in Year 1** to **50 people in Year 5**.

| Year | Projected Team Size |
|---|---:|
| Year 1 | 5 |
| Year 2 | 10 |
| Year 3 | 20 |

Actual hiring should be aligned with product requirements, revenue growth, funding availability, operational needs, and enterprise expansion.

## Growth Assumptions

The projection framework should be evaluated against several operating assumptions:

- User acquisition must increase sufficiently to support the projected growth path.
- Conversion from community or free usage to paid plans must support projected revenue.
- Enterprise adoption may contribute materially to revenue growth.
- Product development and infrastructure must scale with increasing usage.
- Team expansion must remain aligned with business and technical requirements.
- Funding availability must support planned development and market expansion.
- Pricing and packaging assumptions should be reviewed as customer evidence becomes available.

## Funding Alignment

The financial projections should be considered together with the planned investment stages:

- **Seed:** $500K over approximately 6 months for core development and MVP activities.
- **Series A:** $2M over approximately 12 months for scaling and team expansion.
- **Series B:** $5M over approximately 18 months for market expansion.

The funding roadmap and financial projections are related planning documents. Funding amounts and timing should be reassessed as actual development costs, customer traction, operating expenses, and capital requirements become available.

## Key Metrics for Future Validation

The projections should be monitored using measurable operating and financial indicators, including:

- Monthly and annual revenue
- Recurring revenue
- Number of registered users
- Active users
- Paying users
- Customer conversion rate
- Enterprise customers
- Average revenue per customer
- Customer acquisition cost
- Customer retention
- Operating expenses
- Cash runway
- Hiring and personnel costs
- Cloud and infrastructure costs

## Projection Review

Financial projections should be reviewed periodically as actual business data becomes available.

Updates should distinguish clearly between:

- Historical actual results
- Current operating metrics
- Management targets
- Financial forecasts
- Funding assumptions
- Market assumptions

The five-year figures supplied for this phase should therefore serve as a strategic planning baseline rather than a guarantee of future performance.

## Investor Presentation Guidance

When used in investor materials, the projections should be accompanied by the assumptions and evidence supporting the revenue, user-growth, and team-growth trajectory.

Investor-facing materials should avoid presenting these figures as guaranteed outcomes and should clearly identify them as forward-looking planning projections.
