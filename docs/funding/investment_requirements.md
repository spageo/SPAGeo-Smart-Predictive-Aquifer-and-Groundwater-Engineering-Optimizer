# SPAGeo Investment Requirements

## Purpose

The SPAGeo Investment Requirements document defines the planned funding requirements associated with the project's development and commercialization roadmap.

The funding amounts, timelines, and intended uses described below represent planned investment requirements. They are not claims of currently secured funding, active investment commitments, or completed financing rounds.

## Investment Requirements

| Phase | Amount | Timeline | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed | $500K | 6 months | Core development, MVP |
| Series A | $2M | 12 months | Scaling, team expansion |
| Series B | $5M | 18 months | Market expansion, enterprise |

## Seed

**Planned Amount:** $500K
**Planned Timeline:** 6 months

The Seed stage is intended to support core SPAGeo development and MVP delivery.

Potential uses include:

- Core software development
- Groundwater modeling workflow development
- AI-assisted functionality development
- MODFLOW and FloPy integration
- MVP testing and validation
- Initial documentation and developer infrastructure

The Seed requirement represents a planned funding target for the initial development and MVP stage.

## Series A

**Planned Amount:** $2M
**Planned Timeline:** 12 months

The Series A stage is intended to support scaling of the SPAGeo platform and expansion of the development and operational team.

Potential uses include:

- Product and platform scaling
- Engineering and AI team expansion
- Cloud infrastructure development
- Product reliability and performance improvements
- Enterprise-oriented capabilities
- Customer and community support
- Continued research and development

The Series A requirement represents a planned funding target following the initial development and MVP stage.

## Series B

**Planned Amount:** $5M
**Planned Timeline:** 18 months

The Series B stage is intended to support broader market expansion and enterprise growth.

Potential uses include:

- Market expansion
- Enterprise product development
- Cloud and infrastructure scaling
- International and regional market development
- Enterprise support capabilities
- Strategic partnerships
- Expanded engineering, product, and commercial teams

The Series B requirement represents a planned funding target associated with broader commercialization and enterprise expansion.

## Funding Allocation Principles

Planned investment should be allocated according to the development and commercialization priorities of each funding stage.

Key principles include:

1. Prioritize core product development and technical reliability.
2. Maintain alignment between investment and measurable product milestones.
3. Scale infrastructure in accordance with actual usage and customer requirements.
4. Expand the team as technical, product, support, and commercial requirements increase.
5. Allocate funding toward sustainable product and market development.
6. Review funding requirements periodically as project scope and operating conditions evolve.

## Funding Milestones

The planned funding stages correspond to progressively broader SPAGeo development objectives:

**Seed:** Core development and MVP

**Series A:** Scaling and team expansion

**Series B:** Market expansion and enterprise development

Actual financing timing, financing structure, investor participation, valuation, and allocation may differ from this planning framework.

## Review

The investment requirements should be reviewed periodically as SPAGeo's technical roadmap, development costs, infrastructure requirements, team needs, market opportunities, and commercialization strategy evolve.
