# SPAGeo Marketing Strategy

## Purpose

The SPAGeo Marketing Strategy defines the planned approaches for increasing awareness, building the SPAGeo community, reaching potential users and organizations, and supporting the future commercialization of the platform.

The activities described in this document represent planned go-to-market channels and are not claims that all listed programs, partnerships, events, or campaigns are currently active.

## Content Marketing

Content marketing is planned as a primary method for educating users, demonstrating SPAGeo capabilities, and increasing awareness of groundwater modeling and GIS-based aquifer engineering workflows.

Planned content channels include:

### Technical Blog Posts

Technical articles may cover groundwater modeling concepts, MODFLOW workflows, GIS-based aquifer analysis, predictive modeling, AI-assisted groundwater engineering, and practical SPAGeo workflows.

### YouTube Tutorials

Video tutorials may demonstrate SPAGeo installation, project setup, GIS data preparation, model construction, simulation workflows, visualization, and AI-assisted capabilities.

### Case Studies

Case studies may document representative groundwater modeling projects, workflow improvements, model-building approaches, and practical applications of SPAGeo.

Case studies should clearly distinguish illustrative examples, experimental results, and real-world deployments where applicable.

### Webinars

Webinars may be used to introduce SPAGeo capabilities, demonstrate workflows, provide technical training, and engage prospective users and organizations.

## Community Building

Community-building activities are intended to support adoption, technical collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continued development of the SPAGeo ecosystem.

### Open Source Contributions

The project may encourage contributions through source-code development, documentation improvements, issue reporting, testing, examples, model workflows, and technical discussions.

### User Conferences

SPAGeo may participate in or organize user-focused conferences, meetups, workshops, or technical sessions to share project developments and gather feedback from the groundwater modeling and GIS community.

### Academic Partnerships

Academic partnerships may support research collaboration, student projects, educational use, demonstrations, technical validation, and adoption of SPAGeo in academic environments.

### Government Collaborations

Government collaborations may support evaluation and application of SPAGeo for groundwater assessment, aquifer management, environmental analysis, and related public-sector workflows.

Any future government collaboration would be subject to applicable institutional requirements, procurement processes, agreements, and regulatory conditions.

## Sales Channels

The planned sales channels are intended to provide multiple routes for organizations and users to discover, evaluate, and obtain commercial SPAGeo services.

### Direct Sales

Direct sales may be used to engage organizations that require commercial capabilities, enterprise support, consulting services, or deployment arrangements.

### Partner Network

A partner network may extend SPAGeo's reach through organizations providing groundwater consulting, GIS services, engineering services, technology integration, training, or related domain expertise.

### QGIS Plugin Repository

The QGIS plugin ecosystem may provide an additional discovery and distribution channel for SPAGeo functionality that is appropriate for QGIS-based workflows.

Repository distribution, plugin availability, and applicable QGIS requirements would depend on the final packaging and publication process.

### Academic Discounts

Academic discounts may be offered to eligible educational institutions, researchers, instructors, or students as part of the planned commercialization strategy.

Eligibility requirements, discount levels, applicable products, and verification procedures may be defined separately before launch.

## Marketing Principles

SPAGeo marketing should emphasize practical technical value, transparent communication, reproducible workflows, and responsible representation of project capabilities.

Marketing materials should distinguish planned features from released functionality and should avoid presenting experimental results, future capabilities, or illustrative examples as established production outcomes.

Community engagement should remain consistent with the project's open-source development model and contribution practices.

## Review

The marketing strategy should be reviewed periodically as SPAGeo capabilities, target users, community participation, partnerships, commercial offerings, and market conditions evolve.
