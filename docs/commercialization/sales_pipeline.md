# SPAGeo Sales Pipeline

## Purpose

The SPAGeo Sales Pipeline defines the planned customer journey from initial awareness and user acquisition through evaluation, paid adoption, and potential enterprise expansion.

The pipeline represents a planned commercialization framework and is not a claim that all stages, sales processes, customer programs, or commercial services are currently operational.

## Sales Pipeline

The planned high-level customer journey is:

User Acquisition
       |
Free Trial
       |
Pro Upgrade
       |
Enterprise Upsell

Each stage represents a potential transition in the relationship between SPAGeo and a user or organization.

## 1. User Acquisition

User acquisition is the entry point into the SPAGeo ecosystem.

Potential acquisition channels include open-source discovery, technical content, tutorials, webinars, academic outreach, community participation, partner networks, and the QGIS ecosystem.

The objective at this stage is to make potential users aware of SPAGeo and provide accessible information about its capabilities, workflows, and intended use cases.

### Community Path

The Community path is intended for users who begin with the open-source Community Edition.

Users may discover SPAGeo through the repository, documentation, QGIS ecosystem, academic activities, technical content, or community recommendations.

The Community Edition provides an entry point for learning, evaluation, usage, and contribution without requiring a commercial subscription.

## 2. Free Trial

A free-trial stage may provide prospective users with an opportunity to evaluate selected commercial capabilities before purchasing a paid tier.

Trial experiences may include demonstrations or limited access to capabilities associated with the planned Pro or Enterprise offerings, subject to the final commercial packaging.

Trial duration, feature availability, usage limits, eligibility, and access conditions may be defined separately before commercial launch.

### Self-Service Path

The Self-Service path is intended for users who can independently evaluate SPAGeo through documentation, tutorials, trial access, and product information.

This path may allow users to progress from initial discovery to evaluation and, where appropriate, a Pro subscription without direct sales involvement.

## 3. Pro Upgrade

Users who require capabilities beyond the Community Edition or trial experience may transition to the planned Pro tier.

Potential Pro capabilities include AI Copilot functionality and automated model-building workflows, consistent with the planned SPAGeo pricing model.

The decision to upgrade may be supported by product demonstrations, technical documentation, tutorials, usage examples, trial experiences, and other educational materials.

Subscription terms, included capabilities, limits, and pricing remain subject to the final commercial offering.

## 4. Enterprise Upsell

Organizations with broader operational, collaboration, infrastructure, support, or deployment requirements may progress from individual or Pro usage toward the planned Enterprise offering.

Potential Enterprise capabilities include cloud simulation and team collaboration, consistent with the planned SPAGeo pricing model.

Enterprise discussions may also address organizational requirements such as deployment arrangements, support expectations, security considerations, integration needs, and contractual terms.

### Sales-Assisted Path

The Sales-Assisted path is intended for prospective customers that require direct engagement before selecting or expanding a commercial SPAGeo offering.

Sales-assisted engagement may include product demonstrations, requirements discussions, technical consultations, deployment discussions, commercial proposals, and coordination with relevant technical or engineering resources.

## Enterprise Path

The Enterprise path is intended for organizations with requirements that may not be fully addressed through self-service adoption.

Potential activities include:

1. Identification of organizational requirements
2. Technical and workflow evaluation
3. Product demonstration
4. Deployment and integration discussion
5. Commercial proposal
6. Agreement and onboarding
7. Ongoing support and account engagement

The exact enterprise process, contractual terms, service levels, deployment options, and support arrangements may be defined separately for each commercial offering or customer agreement.

## Pipeline Transition Considerations

Movement between pipeline stages should be based on user requirements and demonstrated product needs rather than assuming that every user will progress through every stage.

Possible transitions include:

- Community -> Free Trial
- Community -> Pro Upgrade
- Free Trial -> Pro Upgrade
- Pro Upgrade -> Enterprise Upsell
- Community -> Enterprise evaluation
- Free Trial -> Enterprise evaluation

The actual customer journey may vary according to organization size, technical requirements, deployment preferences, usage patterns, and commercial eligibility.

## Customer Retention and Expansion

After conversion to a paid offering, SPAGeo may support continued customer engagement through product improvements, technical resources, training, consulting, support services, and additional capabilities.

Enterprise customers may have opportunities for broader deployment, additional users, expanded workloads, consulting engagements, or other services where appropriate.

Customer retention and expansion activities should remain aligned with documented product capabilities, contractual commitments, and customer requirements.

## Pipeline Metrics

Future sales operations may track metrics such as:

- User acquisition volume
- Community engagement
- Trial registrations
- Trial-to-Pro conversion
- Pro subscription growth
- Enterprise inquiries
- Enterprise conversion
- Customer retention
- Expansion activity

Specific targets, reporting processes, and measurement definitions may be established separately as the commercial operation develops.

## Review

The sales pipeline should be reviewed periodically as SPAGeo's product capabilities, customer segments, commercial offerings, acquisition channels, and operational processes evolve.
