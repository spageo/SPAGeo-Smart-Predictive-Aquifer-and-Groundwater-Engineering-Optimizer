# SPAGeo Revenue Streams

## Purpose

The SPAGeo Revenue Streams document defines the planned revenue channels supporting the commercialization strategy for the SPAGeo platform.

These revenue streams represent planned business models and are not claims about currently active services, contracts, or commercial offerings.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. SaaS Subscriptions

SPAGeo is planned to offer monthly and annual subscription options for the Pro and Enterprise tiers.

Subscription revenue is intended to support access to additional capabilities and services beyond the Community Edition, including planned AI-assisted workflows, automated model-building capabilities, cloud simulation, and team collaboration features.

Subscription plans, included capabilities, usage limits, service terms, and pricing may evolve before commercial launch.

### 2. Cloud Compute

SPAGeo is planned to support pay-per-simulation cloud execution as a separate revenue stream.

This model is intended to allow users to execute computationally intensive groundwater simulations through cloud infrastructure without requiring all simulation workloads to run on local hardware.

Cloud compute charges may depend on factors such as simulation workload, computational resources, execution duration, storage, and applicable service terms.

### 3. Training Services

SPAGeo is planned to provide paid training services through online courses and workshops.

Training offerings may cover topics such as groundwater modeling, MODFLOW workflows, GIS-based aquifer analysis, SPAGeo workflows, AI-assisted modeling, and related technical practices.

Training formats, course content, delivery methods, schedules, and pricing may be defined separately as the commercial offering develops.

### 4. Consulting

SPAGeo may provide consulting services for organizations requiring custom groundwater-modeling and engineering support.

Consulting engagements may include custom model development, workflow adaptation, project-specific analysis, technical integration, model review, and related engineering services.

Scope, deliverables, timelines, support arrangements, and fees would be defined separately for each engagement.

### 5. Enterprise Licensing

SPAGeo is planned to support enterprise licensing for organizations requiring on-premise enterprise deployments.

Enterprise licensing may provide organizations with deployment arrangements and capabilities appropriate for controlled or private infrastructure environments.

Licensing terms, deployment options, support commitments, security requirements, and associated fees may be defined through individual enterprise agreements.

## Revenue Model Considerations

The revenue streams are intended to complement the open-source Community Edition while supporting continued development, infrastructure, services, and commercial capabilities.

Different revenue streams may be offered independently or combined depending on customer requirements and the final commercial packaging of SPAGeo.

Commercial availability, pricing, service levels, deployment models, and contractual terms remain subject to future product and business decisions.

## Review

The revenue strategy should be reviewed periodically as SPAGeo capabilities, infrastructure costs, customer requirements, market conditions, and commercial offerings evolve.
