# SPAGeo Pricing Model

## Purpose

The SPAGeo Pricing Model defines the planned commercial tiers for the SPAGeo platform and distinguishes the capabilities intended for Community, Pro, Enterprise, and Custom deployments.

The prices and feature allocations below represent the planned commercialization model described in the SPAGeo development roadmap. They are not claims about currently active subscriptions, publicly available offers, or current customer pricing.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Community | Free | Open source core, MODFLOW integration |
| Pro | $99/month | AI Copilot, Automated model building |
| Enterprise | $499/month | Cloud simulation, Team collaboration |
| Custom | Contact | Dedicated support, Private deployment |

## Community

**Price:** Free

The Community tier is intended to provide access to the SPAGeo open-source core and MODFLOW integration for users who want to use, evaluate, and contribute to the open-source project.

## Pro

**Price:** $99/month

The Pro tier is planned to provide additional productivity capabilities, including AI Copilot functionality and automated model-building workflows.

## Enterprise

**Price:** $499/month

The Enterprise tier is planned to provide advanced organizational capabilities, including cloud simulation and team collaboration.

## Custom

**Price:** Contact

The Custom tier is intended for organizations requiring dedicated support and private deployment arrangements.

## Commercialization Notes

The pricing model is part of the planned SPAGeo commercialization strategy. Final pricing, feature availability, service terms, deployment options, support commitments, and applicable taxes or regional pricing may be defined separately before commercial launch.

The Community Edition and its licensing model remain distinct from the planned commercial tiers. The commercial tiers are intended to provide additional services and capabilities without changing the role of the open-source Community Edition.

## Review

The pricing model should be reviewed periodically as SPAGeo capabilities, operating costs, customer requirements, support commitments, and commercial offerings evolve.
